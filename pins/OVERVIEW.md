Pins keeps links and short notes at the top of the Paseo sidebar, similar to pinned chats in a messenger.

Type `/pin https://example.com Optional title` in any composer, or open **Pins verwalten** from the Command Center, to open the pin screen with the entry prefilled. Press **Anpinnen** to save it. Each pin then appears as its own row below the **Angepinnt** row at the top of the sidebar. Pressing a link pin opens it in the browser; pressing a text pin copies it to the clipboard. The X beside a row unpins it.

Pins are stored once per host in the plugin's settings document, so every app connected to the same daemon (desktop, browser, phone) shows the same list and updates live. Nothing is sent anywhere else. Removing the plugin deletes its pins.
